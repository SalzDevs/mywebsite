How I rebuilt a 1987 Model M

A bolt-mod, a sonic cleaner, and a Sunday afternoon. The old keyboard types better now than it did in 1990.

A Model M is a tank with keys. Buckling springs, a steel backplate, and a sound like a tiny storm every time you hit Enter. Mine was yellowed, grimy, and rattling — the classic loose barrel plate problem.

The fix is called a bolt-mod: you drill out the plastic rivets, tap the steel plate, and screw it all back together with bolts. It's tedious, exacting work. Four hours of drill, tap, screw, repeat.

Then you clean the keycaps — a bath of warm water and dish soap does wonders — and put it all back. The first clack is worth every minute.

Old hardware has a way of asking for care. That's the part I like.
